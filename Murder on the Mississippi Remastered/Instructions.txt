After launching x64sc.exe (the modified version found in the Vice_Modded/GTK3VICE-3.10-win32/bin folder), 
go to Preferences > Settings and select Cartridges > Magic Desk. 

Then, select the binary image files for both SRAM and EEPROM (you can find these files in the folder). 

Finally, enable "Enable Magic Desk Plus emulation." 

You can now launch the CRT file.

To access the Save Manager, turn on or reset the Commodore 64 while holding down F1. 
For the Magic Desk Plus Manager, hold down F7.
To bypass the boot process, hold down Run/Stop.
